# Trust model

Fist Commerce is not a trustless credit protocol. The prototype assumes that Fist Commerce and its authorized verification parties perform document review, KYC/KYB, underwriting, legal-assignment checks and other real-world verification correctly.

Soroban verifies that required attestations were issued by authorized addresses and enforces the resulting financing conditions. It does not independently determine whether an invoice, shipment, insurance policy, merchant or legal assignment is genuine.
